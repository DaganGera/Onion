# Audit of the SAMA prototype (branch agent/frontier-2258)

Read on 24 Sep 2026 before any new code. The old tree now lives in `legacy/` so it stays readable; nothing there runs in the new app.

## What we kept

- The statistics ideas in `legacy/app/grading.py`: Wilson intervals, reporting "bulb-observations" instead of onions, and taking the least favourable look instead of pooling looks. They were ported to TypeScript with golden-vector tests (`packages/core/test/golden.test.ts`, 40 random `merge_looks` cases plus 41 Wilson cases generated by `tools/make_golden.py`). All pass.
- The printed calibration mat: A4 landscape, eight 40 mm DICT_4X4_50 markers, IDs 0-7 (`legacy/app/mat_layout.py`). The new detector reads it through the vendored js-aruco2 with OpenCV's 4x4 dictionary. `calibration_mat.pdf` ships with the app.
- The idea of an append-only hash chain and a public verify page, rebuilt on RFC 8785 canonical JSON, device signatures and an RFC 6962 Merkle log.
- The two-look protocol (shake the tray, look again).

## What we dropped, and why

- The YOLO26 detector and its weights (`yolo26*.pt`, `weights/best.pt`, 90 MB). It was trained and scored only on synthetic trays (the old STATUS.md says so), boxes cannot give the per-bulb area fractions the norms are written in, and Ultralytics is AGPL-3.0, which the brief rules out of the runtime path.
- `scripts/make_synthetic.py` and every metric derived from it (mAP 0.989, occlusion 20.5%/10.8%, height correction 0.9267). None of these may be quoted.
- The fitted constants in `app/constants.json` for the same reason.
- `cloudflared.exe` (53 MB binary in git) and the tunnel-based phone demo. The new app is offline-first and needs no tunnel.
- The FastAPI server as the place where grading happens. Grading now runs in the browser from one TypeScript core; the verifier re-runs the same code.
- The ICAR-DOGR size bands (A >= 80 mm, B >= 50 mm, C >= 30 mm) hard-coded in `grading.py`. They do not match the 2026 PSF procurement norms and had no source. Sizes now come from rule packs.
- The `.opencode/` multi-agent loop configuration and `.agent/` logs: process artefacts, not product.

## Unverified claims in the old docs and slides

- "96%+ defect detection accuracy", "24,000 quintals/day", "~90% loss reduction" (Agrograde, NITI Aayog case study, slide table): not checked; not used.
- "98.2% accuracy" (FQAN-PROA), "77.30% mAP50" (YOLO-ODD): numbers from other papers on other data; not comparable to our task.
- "holdout mAP50 0.989", "GPU 20 ms p95", "1-look misses 20.5%": synthetic.
- `.agent/REAL_DATA.md` says 4,800 Zenodo images were ingested with boxes from colour segmentation. Those files are not in the repo (`data/` is gitignored), so nothing downstream can be reproduced.
- The slide's "SHA-256 encryption": SHA-256 is a hash, not encryption. Our receipts are signed, and we describe them as tamper-evident.
- "PS 26046" vs "26031": open (OPEN_QUESTIONS 9).

## Bugs and risks found

1. `grading.py` applied `height_correction` as a single constant, fitted on synthetic images. The correction depends on camera height and bulb size; the new code computes it per bulb from the calibration (`core/sizing.ts: heightCorrect`).
2. `merge_looks` reported one Wilson interval over all bulb-observations, which treats bulbs in the same tray as independent and pools two looks of the same onions. The new lot statistics resample whole trays and use one look per tray.
3. The REFER rule was "model confidence < 0.75". A confident model can still be within measuring error of a size limit. The new rule sends a bulb to a human when any rule is undecided within its measurement interval.
4. The old verify page trusted the server's copy of the grade. The new verifier recomputes it.
5. Risk: all tolerances are from press reports. Mitigation: packs are data, versioned and hashed; changing a norm is a new pack, not new code.
6. Risk: Tier-0 colour rules were tuned on one public dataset from one phone. Mitigation: every threshold is in `packages/vision/src/config.ts`, whose hash is printed on each receipt, and the field studies in docs/HUMAN_TASKS.md produce the numbers that replace them.
