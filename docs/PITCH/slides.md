# Ten-slide outline

Numbers on slide 8 must be copied from docs/EVALUATION.md after `node tools/gen_eval_md.mjs`, never typed from memory.

1. **The dispute.** One photo: a farmer and an officer at a NAFED centre looking at the same sack. Headline: "Same onions, two grades." One line: in 2026 the norms changed twice in eight weeks (4 Jun relaxation, 30 Jul URS stopped), and each centre applied them by eye.

2. **What the norms actually say.** Quote the press text: "up to 30% blackening, up to 40% spots, up to 10% sunburn". Point: these are shares of a bulb's surface. A box detector cannot measure a share. Parakh measures it.

3. **Parakh in one line.** An instrument, a rulebook, a receipt. Three icons, three sentences, nothing else.

4. **The instrument.** Screenshot of the Capture Guard (six checks) and one bulb with its mask. "34.2% blackening, limit 30%": the reason code under it. Every number has an uncertainty; within error of a limit goes to a person.

5. **The lot, not the bulb.** Screenshot of the result screen: decision, procurable share by weight with its interval, the sequential-test advice. Two sentences: we resample whole trays, never single onions; a second look is never counted as new onions. The sack draw uses codes from both farmer and officer.

6. **The rulebook.** The timeline of five rule packs (15 May, 4 Jun variants A and B, 30 Jul). Screenshot of time travel: same lot, five packs, five results. Norms change without an app update.

7. **The receipt.** QR on the receipt, second phone in airplane mode showing six green checks, then the tampered code failing. "Tamper-evident, not tamper-proof": say it on the slide.

8. **What we measured, honestly.** The generated table from docs/EVALUATION.md: public real-photo results for Tier 0 and Tier 1, browser checks, and E1 to E4 (field studies) with their status. If E2 and E3 are done, lead with them: the human baseline is the number that answers "is it less subjective than people?"

9. **Why it holds up in a centre.** Offline, installs as an app, costs a printed A4 mat and a phone the centre already has; nine languages drafted; every dependency permissive. One line on privacy: the farmer reference stays on the phone.

10. **Ask and next steps.** Pilot at two centres for one week: field photos, the three studies, then retrain. Show the one-slide limitations list next to it. Team AlgoRangersV1.
