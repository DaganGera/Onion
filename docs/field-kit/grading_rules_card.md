# Grading card (default rule pack: 30 Jul 2026, Grade A only, press-report)

Judge each bulb by what you can see. Size is the smallest width across the middle.

Reject if any of these: rot or wet lesion, sprouting, deep cut, size under 35 mm or over 70 mm, blackening over 30% of the surface, spots over 40%, sunburn over 10%, split bulb.

Grade A if all of these: size 45 to 65 mm, blackening at most 5%, spots at most 10%, sunburn at most 2%, peeled skin at most 10%, not double, no bottleneck, not split.

URS: everything else that is not Reject (it is measured but, since 30 Jul 2026, reportedly not bought).

? : you cannot decide. That is an allowed answer.

The Grade A limits for blackening, spots, sunburn and peel are placeholders (docs/SOURCES.md A-GRADEA-1). Use them anyway so humans and the app follow the same rules.
