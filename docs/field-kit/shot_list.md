# Shot list (about 350 photos)

Use the app's camera for everything you can; it records the calibration tier and runs the Capture Guard. Keep the printed mat (or a plain A4 sheet) in every photo unless the line says otherwise.

## Day 1, place 1 (training set)

| # | Scene | Light | Background | Photos |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6-8 sound bulbs, spread, on the mat | shade outdoors | mat | 20 |
| 2 | same, touching each other in a single layer | shade | mat | 20 |
| 3 | 30-40 bulbs in a tray, mat beside the tray | shade | tray | 30 |
| 4 | cull pile bulbs, spread, defect side up | shade | mat | 30 |
| 5 | cull pile bulbs, defect side down (then shake: second look) | shade | mat | 30 |
| 6 | mixed good and cull, in a tray | direct sun | tray | 20 |
| 7 | mixed, in a tray | indoor tube light | tray | 20 |
| 8 | white onions on the mat and on a dark cloth | shade | both | 20 |
| 9 | plain A4 sheet instead of the mat | shade | sheet | 15 |
| 10 | a Rs 10 coin instead of the mat | shade | table | 10 |
| 11 | no reference at all | shade | table | 10 |

## Day 2, place 2, another phone if possible (holdout set, never used for training)

Repeat rows 3, 4, 6 and 8 with 20 photos each (80 photos).

## Rules

- Phone about 30 to 50 cm above the table, pointing straight down.
- Every bulb whole in the frame; the app ignores bulbs cut off by the edge.
- Note which numbered bulbs are in each photo when you will need them for T4 to T6.
