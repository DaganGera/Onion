Demo script

  1. Pick any certificate below and press ATTACK — this really edits its Grade A value straight in the database, +25 points, exactly like a dishonest operator with database access would. No hashes are recomputed.
  2. The chain check flags that exact record red — and its certificate page shows TAMPERED to anyone who scans its QR code.
  3. Press RESTORE to put the original value back and watch it go green again.

Honest limits: someone with full database access could recompute the whole chain — that is why this is called tamper-evident, not tamper-proof, and why both parties sign on their own devices as well.
