Method. Bulb sizes measured against a printed eight-marker ArUco calibration mat (40.0 mm squares) lying in the same plane as the tray, graded to ICAR-DOGR bands. Where markers were obscured, scale was recovered from whatever reference remained and the reduced quality is stated above. The Grade A proportion is a sample estimate with a 95% Wilson confidence interval, not a census of the lot. Bulbs below the confidence threshold are referred to a human inspector rather than auto-graded. When a lot was photographed in more than one look, the interval counts each physical onion once — the "distinct bulbs" figure is that effective n — because repeat sightings of the same bulb are not independent evidence. Surface-visible defects only: internal rot behind sound skin cannot be detected from photographs and is not counted in the defect figures below its visible forms. The defect rate is estimated from the least-occluded view of each tray; its interval describes sampling error on that view.
